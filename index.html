The soil surfactant portfolio, product by product

Each HydroForce surfactant has its own job. Here is how the nine products in the portfolio line up.

  • Granular · residualCalWet Granular

    A residual surfactant in granular form, developed specifically for fine turf such as greens and sportsfields.

  • DewDewPRO

    A non-ionic surfactant that helps turf managers deal with dew formation in cooler conditions.

  • PenetrantEvolve Soil Penetrant

    An anionic soil-amending additive that changes the surface tension of water bound in the soil.

  • Long-termExtend

    A long-term surfactant for water repellency that promotes turf uniformity for up to 90 days.

  • IrrigationInject

    A non-ionic surfactant blend for irrigation efficiency, applied through irrigation systems or conventional sprayers.

  • PenetrantRapid

    A highly concentrated penetrant surfactant that moves soil moisture further into the profile.

  • ResidualRecharge

    A highly concentrated residual surfactant aimed at water repellency and localised dry spot.

  • Co-polymerUltra

    Built on modified block co-polymer surfactant technology to increase the wettability of treated surfaces.

  • Granular · DGTGranular Greens

    Pairs a high-performance surfactant with dispersible granule technology (DGT).

Always read and follow the product label. Full specifications are on the HydroForce page of the Indigo website.

From golf and bowling greens to sportsfields

CalWet Granular was developed for fine turf such as greens and sportsfields. Granular Greens takes a different route, combining a surfactant with dispersible granule technology.

For liquid programs, Inject goes out through irrigation systems or conventional sprayers, while Evolve and Rapid are the penetrants in the range. Recharge and Extend are the longer-acting options for water repellency.

Not sure which fits your site? Indigo's TechForce diagnostic service can look at the problem first, working with testing partners Westgate Labs, Ground Science, Plant Science Consulting and Tweed Laboratories.

HydroForce for ponds and water features

Three HydroForce products sit in Indigo's Aquatic Management Portfolio, for managing algae, weed, sludge and odour in water bodies.

  • Aqua Blue: a highly concentrated aquatic colourant that turns off-coloured water a natural blue in ponds, fountains and water hazards.
  • Bio-Blast Aqua Pucks: a microbial blend designed for waste water treatment, breaking down organic debris and sediment.
  • Bio-Blast WSP: a microbially based blend aimed at water clarity and odour, and at tying up nutrients to help prevent algal growth.

The company behind HydroForce

HydroForce is one of several ranges from Indigo Specialty Products Pty Ltd, a privately owned business at Rocklea, in Brisbane's south. The people behind it come from manufacturing, distribution, product development and registration roles, and their customers sit across the Australasian non-crop sector and specialised corners of horticulture. Indigo makes its ranges in Australia where possible, and tests formulations in field trials here so they are suited to Australian conditions.

Alongside HydroForce, Indigo supplies ProForce plant protection, BioForce biologicals, NutriForce fertilisers, IVM vegetation management products and consumer small packs, plus the TechForce diagnostic service.
