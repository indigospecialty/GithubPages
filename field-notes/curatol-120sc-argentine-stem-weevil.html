Argentine Stem Weevil on two Western Sydney sites: a preventative program with ProForce Curatol 120SC

Indigo Specialty Products · 19 November 2026 · 3 minute read

First published by Indigo Specialty Products on 9 April 2026. Read the original post.

On some turf sites, Argentine Stem Weevil turns up season after season. Steve Konkolics of Superior Turf Services looks after two of them in Western Sydney, and he told Indigo Specialty Products how last season went after a change to his program.

What Steve said

“I manage two sites in Western Sydney that have historically struggled with Argentine Stem Weevil. This season, I added ProForce Curatol 120SC to my preventative program during the peak activity period of December and January and had no issues. I'll continue using it and would recommend it to others.”

Steve Konkolics, Superior Turf Services

Points from the program

  • He planned ahead. Curatol 120SC went into a preventative program rather than being used after damage appeared.
  • He targeted the busiest months. December and January are the peak activity period on his sites, so that is where the applications were focused.
  • The sites had a history. Both had struggled with the weevil before, the kind of situation where a planned program tends to earn its place.

Peak activity can shift with region and season, so set your timing from your own monitoring records. Rates, timing and registered uses are all on the product label.
